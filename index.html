Follow the instructions below for recording your 360-degree video:

  • 3. Position yourself in the environment you want to record.
  • 4. Press the record button to start recording.
  • 5. The video will record for 25 seconds.
  • 6. After recording, the app will display a QR code.
  • 7. Scan the QR code with your phone to view the video.
